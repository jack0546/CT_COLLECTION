How Delivery Works

1. Order Confirmation. Once you place your order via WhatsApp, our team confirms stock availability and sends you a final invoice with your delivery fee included.

2. Payment. You pay via Mobile Money or choose Cash on Delivery (available for select areas).

3. Dispatch. Your order is packed and handed to our delivery partner within 24 hours. You receive a tracking reference by SMS or WhatsApp.

4. Delivery. Our rider calls you before arriving. Please ensure someone is available to receive the package.

Packaging

Every order is wrapped in protective packaging to keep your items clean, folded, and damage-free during transit. If you are buying a gift, let us know on WhatsApp and we will include a complimentary gift bag at no extra charge.

Delivery Questions

Can I change my delivery address after ordering?

Yes, if your order has not yet been dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp as soon as possible with your order reference and the new address. Once dispatched, address changes may not be possible.

What happens if I miss my delivery?

Our rider will call you before arriving. If no one is available, we will attempt delivery again the next business day. After two failed attempts, the order may be returned to us and additional delivery fees may apply.

Do you deliver outside Ghana?

Currently, we only deliver within Ghana. For international orders, please contact us on WhatsApp to discuss custom shipping arrangements.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Please inspect your package when it arrives and contact us immediately with photos of any damage. We will arrange a replacement or refund within 48 hours of receiving your report.
