Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering from CT Collection.

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our shop, select your size and color, and click the "Order via WhatsApp" button. This will open a chat with us where we confirm availability, delivery time, and payment details.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery within Accra typically takes 24–48 hours. For other regions in Ghana, it usually takes 2–4 business days. We will give you an exact timeframe when you place your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo) and Cash on Delivery. Once your order is confirmed on WhatsApp, we will send you the payment details.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes! If your item doesn't fit or isn't what you expected, you can exchange it within 7 days of receiving it, provided it is unworn and in its original condition.
